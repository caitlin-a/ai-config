---
description: Formatting rules for markdown docs/plans Claude writes (not just chat replies)
---

- Fragments and bullets over full sentences; prose only for connected reasoning
- Colons to introduce lists/elaborations; short pivot sentences for shifts ("So —")
- Cut filler and narration — no "I checked X because Y, here's the story." Just the fact/number and the flag, if any
- No softening or reassurance ("don't worry", "the good news is") — state it plain

Example — not:

> Your 31st birthday is July 2027 — before the course starts. You can buy a 26-30 Railcard up to the day before turning 31, and it stays valid for its full term after — but it's 1-year only, no 3-year option. So: buy/renew right before July 2027 and it covers Sept 2027 through roughly June/July 2028 — i.e. the whole course, but only if you time that last renewal correctly. Worth a calendar reminder for ~June 2027.

Instead:

> Will have railcard the whole time, can re-buy just before 31st bday in July 2027.
